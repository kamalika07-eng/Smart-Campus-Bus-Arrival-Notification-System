# 📖 Project Overview

Many college students rely on college buses for their daily commute. However, traffic congestion, weather conditions, and unexpected delays often make buses arrive earlier or later than the scheduled time. Since students only know the fixed timetable, they may miss the bus or spend a long time waiting at the bus stop.

This project proposes a simple and affordable notification system that informs students when their bus is about to reach their stop, helping them manage their time effectively and reduce missed buses.

---

# 🚨 Problem Statement

Students frequently miss college buses because they do not receive real-time updates about bus arrival times.

### Current Challenges

- No real-time bus arrival information
- Unexpected traffic delays
- Students wait unnecessarily at bus stops
- Late arrival to college
- Increased transportation expenses
- Frequent complaints to the transport office
- Stress for students and parents

# 💡 Proposed Solution

A simple system that informs students when their college bus is about to reach their stop so they can be ready on time.

---

# ⚙️ How It Works

1. The bus starts its route.
2. The driver shares the bus location.
3. The system updates the current bus status.
4. Students receive arrival notifications.
5. Students reach the bus stop before the bus arrives.
6. Students board the bus on time.
